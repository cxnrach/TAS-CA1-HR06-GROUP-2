Overview

Job Overview:

  • Barista plays a key role in Starbuck’s success through delivering excellent customer services to all customers. From preparing quality beverages and food to maintaining a clean and welcoming store environment, the role requires teamwork, adaptability, and the passion that models and acts in accordance with Starbucks guiding principles.
Purpose

Purpose of a Barista Role:

  • Delivering exceptional customer service to every customer
  • Creating meaningful customer connections and positive experiences
  • Preparing beverages and food products according to Starbucks standards
  • Supporting smooth daily store operations in a fast-paced retail environment
  • Promoting Starbucks’ culture, mission, and values through teamwork and professionalism
Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities:

  • Consistently deliver exceptional customer service by putting customers first, understanding and anticipating their needs, and maintaining a calm, positive attitude to ensure smooth store operations even during busy or challenging situations.
  • Prepare and serve high-quality food and beverages according to standard or customised recipes while consistently following presentation, health, safety, and sanitation standards.
  • Follow operational, cash handling, safety, and security procedures while maintaining a clean and organised workspace and being flexible in performing various tasks during each shift.
  • Support new partner training through positive reinforcement and constructive coaching while contributing to a positive team environment by recognising and communicating changes in partner morale and performance.
  • Act with integrity, honesty and knowledge that promote the culture, values and mission of Starbucks.
Job Requirements

Job Requirements:

  • At least 16 years old and above
  • No previous experience required
  • Available to work flexible hours that may include early mornings, evenings, weekends, nights and/or public holidays
  • Maintain regular and consistent attendance and punctuality
Skill Requirements

Required Knowledge, Skills and Abilities:

  • Warm, welcoming, and passionate about delivering excellent customer service
  • Strong communication and teamwork skills with the ability to build respectful relationships
  • Quick learner who is highly motivated and able to follow oral and written instructions effectively
